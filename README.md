# Our Recipes!

WoW Classic addon for browsing and sharing who in your guild knows which recipes and enchants.

## Our Installation

Drop the addon folder into `Interface/AddOns/`.

## Our Instructions

`/or` or `/ourrecipes` to open the window.

Open a profession window in-game to scan your own known recipes automatically. Export your data and import the guild's from the import/export tab to see the full guild roster's recipes.
